*In 2010, the NUTS2 classification distinguished between FI1A (Itä-Suomi) and FI13 (Pohjois-Suomi) as separate regions. In 2020, these regions were aggregated into a single region FI1D (FI1A + FI13 = FI1D) in the IFS data. However, since the model structure maintains the 2010 regional classification, we assign the entire FI1D quantity to FI130000. Consequently, FI1A0000 contains no values in the final dataset.
